For Izcal, the durable finding is the stability penalty and the verification tax: agentic implementation must be budgeted with extra review, testing and rollback, not as a pure reduction of engineer-hours.

## 2. GitClear code-quality analyses

GitClear publishes line-level repository telemetry. It consistently finds declining refactoring, rising copy/paste and rising churn, but **none of its public reports attributes individual commits to AI**; the AI link is temporal/correlational. Treat the numbers as a real trend in large repositories, not as an AI effect size.

## 8. What the industry data cannot establish

- **No causal enterprise estimate.** DORA, Stack Overflow and Anthropic are observational; the Faros company-level analysis finds no effect; the GitHub/Accenture trial is small, vendor-run and does not disclose its sample. There is no published randomised enterprise-wide estimate of delivery throughput or time-to-merge under agentic development.
- **No AI attribution in the code-quality data.** GitClear's AI link is temporal. Its churn, duplication and refactoring trends are real measurements of repositories, but not measured AI effects.
- **No independent time-to-merge series.** Spotify publishes merged-PR volume but not cycle time. Faros measures review time and PR size, not merge time. GitHub/Accenture measures PR count and merge rate. The brief's "time-to-merge" question is therefore unanswered by public data as of 21 October 2026.
- **Vendor selection and interest.** Every enterprise case in section 5 is vendor-authored or vendor-commissioned, with no audit and, in most cases, no baseline. The claims are useful as upper bounds on what is possible with a clear specification, not as averages.
- **Self-report and sample skew.** DORA and Stack Overflow measure what respondents say; Stack Overflow's respondents are a self-selected subset of its audience; Anthropic's samples are its own users, not the developer population.
- **No agent-hours or tokens per engineering task at engine scale.** The only task-time figures found are Anthropic's model-estimated session values (section 4.2). There is no published measurement of agent-hours or token spend per merged change in a multi-year, multi-million-line systems project.
- **Adoption definitions differ.** "Use or plan to use" (Stack Overflow 2024), "use at work" (DORA 2025) and "weekly active" (Faros) are not the same denominator and must not be compared directly.

## 9. Implications for the activity-class translation (evidence, not the model)

The frame requires a per-activity-class translation, not a flat multiplier. The industry evidence sorts as follows. The confidence column grades the evidence, not any Izcal factor.

| Activity class | What the industry data says | Direction for compression | Evidence conf. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implementation / code writing | Vendor project claims 2–10×; Faros team-level tasks +21% and PRs +98%; Anthropic Claude makes about 80% of execution decisions; DORA 2024 individual productivity self-report +2.1% per 25% adoption | Compressible, but measured organisation-level gains are far below vendor claims; use a range, not a point | Medium |
| Review / verification | Faros review time +91%, PR size +154%; Stack Overflow 45% slower debugging; DORA 2024 stability −7.2% despite review speed +3.1%; Spotify reports review pressure | Does not compress; likely expands per unit of generated code | Medium–High |
| Testing / QA | Faros time in QA +300.6%; Veracode 45% of tests found AI-introduced flaws; vendor claims of large test-generation gains are unaudited | May expand unless test generation is itself agentic; unproven at scale | Medium |
| Planning / architecture / specification | Claude Code users make about 70% of planning decisions; DORA's stability penalty is tied to batch size and design discipline; Spotify describes review and rollout pressure | Incompressible human decision bandwidth | Medium |
| Long-lived maintenance / refactoring | GitClear refactoring 25% → <10% (2024) and 21% → 3.8% (2026 YTD); long-term updates −74%; duplication and churn rising | At risk of degradation; may add future cost rather than save time | Medium |
| Incidents / operations | Spotify finds no material direct AI-code cause so far; Faros incidents per PR +14.5% and monthly incidents +125.4%; Replit data-deletion incident | Uncertain; treat as a monitored risk, not a saving | Low–Medium |

**Implications:** The evidence supports two conclusions for the C-6 restatement. First, implementation and execution are the only classes with credible compression, and the credible range is wide. Second, verification, review, QA, planning and maintenance show either no compression or expansion, and DORA 2024 plus GitClear show that compressing implementation without those classes can reduce delivery stability and code health. Any translation should therefore carry a range marked †, keep the incompressible classes near 1× or above, and be restated at Izcal's first measured agentic milestones. No third-party source code was read for this note; all evidence is published prose and data.
