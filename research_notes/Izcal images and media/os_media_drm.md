# Izcal images and media: os_media_drm

Compiled 19 October 2026. Primary sources were preferred (papers, specifications, maintainers' posts, release notes, issue trackers).
Confidence: **High** = primary source read and the claim checked in its text; **Medium** = primary abstract or snippet, or a vendor claim without data; **Low** = secondary source only.

**Method notes.**
- Scope: what each operating system offers as a media, DRM, audio and capture interface, as its vendor documents it; codec patent pools and their published terms; DRM licensing routes for an independent engine. Izcal builds its own decoders (M-1); this note covers only what may be called as an OS interface (HGBA-2 2.3.2, licence-audit class 5) and the patent facts behind the decision in Stage 1 13.2 "Codecs".
- No source code of any browser, codec library or driver was read. Apple documentation was read through its published documentation JSON (`developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/...json`), which is the same text as the rendered page. One Chromium page cited (5.4) is the prose `README.md` of the audio service, not code.
- Izcal's own DRM spike results (`m0/drm-spike/results/*.jsonl`, 14 October 2026) were re-parsed for this note (Section 7).
- The session's shared web-search budget ran out after about 15 searches. Later items were read by direct fetches of known primary URLs. Rows taken from the 14 October 2026 fact-check (`research_notes/Stage 1 design facts/scope_completion_factcheck.md`) and not re-fetched here are graded Medium.
- This note records facts for a legal decision. It is not legal advice. Every royalty question below is for the codec legal review required before M7 (Stage 1 13.2).
