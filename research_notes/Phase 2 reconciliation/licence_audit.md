# Licence audit under the MIT-only rule

*Audit of 19 October 2026, on branch `spec/phase2-engine`. The audit itself edited no spec. **The owner decided every question on 19 October 2026 (OD-L3 to OD-L9 below); the decisions are applied in HGBA-2 2.3.2, the style spec 0.3.2, the fonts and text spec 0.3.3, the networking spec 0.3.2 and the reconciliation note's Section 7.** Sources: the JavaScript engine, resource footprint, HTML and CSS parsing and style, fonts and text, and networking specs; the ML service and phishing protection specs (batch 4, 21 October 2026); HGBA-2 M-1 part 1; the Stage 1 design (13.1, 13.2, D-S6, D-S10); CONTRIBUTING.md; `tools/deny.toml`; every `Cargo.toml` in the repository. Licences were checked on 19 October 2026 against each project's own LICENSE file, its crates.io licence field, or its official licence page (URLs below). A dependency tree for `rustls` 0.23.46 with `aws-lc-rs` was resolved with `cargo metadata` to see what it really links.*

*Revision (21 October 2026, branch `spec/phase2c`): batch-4 additions, requested by the ML service spec 0.3 §9.5 and the phishing protection spec 0.3 §9.5. The audit gains **class 6**, model weights and their training data, with the ML spec's D0–D5 vocabulary (ML-D1, ML-D3, ML-20); and a **phishing feed table** under PH-D14, which the owner resolved as its recommendation (a) on 21 October 2026: Phishunt (CC0) ships now; CERT Polska and URLAbuse only if this audit's MIT-redistribution test is confirmed with a NOTICE; PhishTank, URLhaus and the Tier 2 feed are test-only or exception-only. The audit classifies CERT Polska, Phishunt, URLAbuse, Phishing.Database, PhishTank, URLhaus/Spamhaus, APWG eCX and Netcraft, with OpenPhish, Phishing Army and Google's lists recorded alongside. **Owner confirmation (21 October 2026, the residue decisions): A-LA-1, A-LA-2 and A-LA-3 are confirmed as recommended** — the table rule (no table taken from RFC code components; per-publisher confirmation of codec constant tables), the Vulkan loader/SPIR-V/ash dispositions, and the content-blocking dataset dispositions (non-commercial datasets unused even test-only until Q-CB-1; Tranco licence-reviewed; entity-data inputs licence-reviewed). **No existing row (OD-L2, OD-L5, OD-L6) authorises shipping a phishing feed.** Neither addition creates a class 2 item: the ML runtime is Izcal's own, and the phishing bundle is expressions and provenance, not a copy of a licensed database. The 19 October decisions are unchanged; Q-ML-9, Q-ML-10 and Q-ML-11 remain open, so the D2, D3 and third-party-MIT branches below are recorded as conditional.*

## The rule

**Owner rule (19 October 2026):** "lets make a mit only, we dont want to be restricted in any way or by any measure, and if so we build our own." Everything Izcal ships must be MIT-licensed, or dual-licensed with MIT as one option (taken under MIT). Anything else that ships, Izcal builds.

**Owner decisions on this audit (19 October 2026):**

- **OD-L1. Unicode data may ship.** The UCD, CLDR, emoji data and other Unicode, Inc. data under the Unicode License v3 are allowed, with no build-our-own.
- **OD-L2. Test-only inputs may have any licence.** WPT, test262, oracles, test data and test tools that never ship are allowed, whatever their licence.
- **OD-L3. TLS and cryptography (Q-L1, Q-L2).** Izcal writes its own TLS 1.3 stack; rustls is out, because it always links rustls-webpki and `untrusted` (ISC) and `subtle` (BSD-3-Clause). Cryptography primitives come from MIT-dual RustCrypto crates; Izcal writes its own X25519 and Ed25519 (the dalek crates are BSD-3-Clause) and anything else not available under MIT. rustls, BoringSSL and other stacks may be black-box test oracles only. Applied: networking 0.3.2, NW-D22 and NW-D23.
- **OD-L4. Certificate trust (Q-L3).** An Izcal root program: Izcal's own root store and policy, audit review, inclusion and distrust process, staffed before NW-M2; Mozilla's CCADB data is not shipped. Applied: NW-D5, with the bootstrapping stated and a development-only OS-anchor interim argued from first principles (NW-D24).
- **OD-L5. HSTS preload list and content-blocking lists (Q-L6).** Izcal builds its own; users may still add third-party lists themselves. Applied: NW-D25, NW-D26, NW-D17.
- **OD-L6. Standards data (Q-L5).** The Public Suffix List, the CT log list, WHATWG tables, the OpenType registries, tz and IANA registries are allowed as standards data, like Unicode data under OD-L1. Applied: every Phase 2 spec's M-1 section uses one wording for it.
- **OD-L7. Fonts (Q-L4).** Izcal designs its own font set (pan-script faces, metric-compatible aliases, math, emoji, last resort) as a type-design program; until a script has an Izcal face, OS fonts render it. Noto, Liberation and STIX are not shipped (black-box test material only). Applied: fonts and text 0.3.3, TX-D24 and TX-D25.
- **OD-L8. Generic crates.** Each spec's M-1 section lists only MIT-available crates and notes the non-MIT defaults found here (2.7) and how they are avoided. Applied in ST, TX and NW Section 7.
- **OD-L9. Policy text (Q-L7).** CONTRIBUTING.md "License" and "Original code", HGBA-2 M-1 part 1 (2.3.2) and `tools/deny.toml` (`[licenses]` allow list `MIT` and `Unicode-3.0`, dev-dependencies excluded) carry the rule. Stage 1 D-S6 consequence (a) is superseded; its text amendment is listed in the specs' amendment sections, not applied.

## Summary

- **The current policy files did not enforce the rule** when this audit was written: `tools/deny.toml` had no `[licenses]` section, CONTRIBUTING.md ("License") still allowed Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, Zlib, Unicode and MPL-2.0, and Stage 1 D-S6 (a) still expected MPL-2.0 dependencies. **Since resolved (OD-L9):** `tools/deny.toml` and CONTRIBUTING.md now carry the MIT-only rule; D-S6 (a)'s amendment is listed, not applied.
- **Nine shipped items fail the rule (class 2).** Two are large: the **TLS crypto provider** (`aws-lc-rs`/AWS-LC, Apache-2.0 OR ISC plus OpenSSL-derived code) and the **Izcal font set** (Noto and the other faces are SIL OFL 1.1). The others are `rustls`'s required dependencies (`rustls-webpki` and `untrusted` under ISC, `subtle` under BSD-3-Clause), Mozilla's root store through CCADB (CDLA-Permissive-2.0), the HSTS preload list (Chromium's source, BSD-3-Clause), content-blocking filter lists (EasyList: GPL-3.0 or CC BY-SA 3.0), and generic crates that are not MIT (for example `foldhash`, Zlib, which `hashbrown` pulls in by default).
- **One spec claim was wrong** (corrected in networking 0.3.2). NW 6.5, Section 7 and NW-D19 said `rustls-webpki` is "not linked". `rustls` 0.23.46 depends on it unconditionally (resolved tree below). It becomes optional only on rustls `main` (0.24-dev, `default = ["tracing", "webpki"]`). `subtle` (BSD-3-Clause) stays mandatory there too.
- **Six shipped standards-data sets are not MIT but are the standard (class 4).** They are the tz database (public domain), IANA registries and BCP 47 (CC0 1.0), WHATWG data (`entities.json`, Encoding index files, the UA style sheet: CC BY 4.0, BSD-3-Clause where incorporated into code), the Public Suffix List (MPL-2.0), the OpenType tag registries (CC BY 4.0 in Microsoft's docs repository; partly verified), and the CT log list (licence not stated; unverified). Building "our own" would mean diverging from the standard. These need an owner answer. Unicode data is in this class too, but OD-L1 has decided it.
- **Every test-only input is allowed under OD-L2 (class 3).** Many are not MIT (WPT and test262 BSD-3-Clause, the Wasm reference interpreter and SpecTec Apache-2.0, `insta` Apache-2.0, and others).
- **Model weights are data sets, so the rule's second limb is provenance (class 6, 21 October 2026).** A shipped weight must be MIT; its training data must be D0 commissioned or D1 MIT. D2 (CC0/public domain) waits on Q-ML-9 and D3 (CC BY) on Q-ML-10, both open with recommendations yes and no; D4 is evaluation-only under OD-L2, and D5 is quarantined. A third-party MIT weight without a documented data chain needs Q-ML-11 (open, recommendation no by default); a non-MIT weight is test-only. No framework ships, so the class adds no build item — the cost is the corpus and compute, already in ML-D3.
- **Of the phishing feeds, only Phishunt is cleared to ship today (21 October 2026).** Phishunt's CC0 is a public-domain dedication and passes the MIT-redistribution test. CERT Polska's "any use" and URLAbuse's CC BY 4.0 pass only if counsel confirms they permit redistribution of the data under MIT with a NOTICE. Phishing.Database is MIT but stale. PhishTank (share-alike), URLhaus (non-commercial and consent-gated), OpenPhish, Phishing Army and Google's lists are test-only; APWG eCX and Netcraft are exception-only under PH-D14 (b). No pre-existing row authorises shipping any feed.
- **The toolchain and OS interfaces cannot practically be replaced (class 5).** These are LLVM inside `rustc`, the C runtimes, the OS SDKs, the graphics, media and accessibility APIs, the drivers, and the DRM modules of D-S7. Rust itself and `std` are MIT OR Apache-2.0 (class 1).
- **Cost of class 2, if everything is built:** about **9–17 engineer-years† once**, plus **8–15 people† ongoing** (crypto, root program, filter lists, HSTS list), plus a **pan-Unicode font set of 150–400 designer-years† over 5–10 years**. The font set dominates, and needs an owner decision (Q-L4).

† marks estimates set by this audit, not measured.

## Class 6: model weights and training data (added 21 October 2026)

*Registered by the ML service spec 0.3 §9.5 (ML-D1, ML-D3, ML-20). A weight file is a data set, and the MIT-only rule covers data as well as code; the same reading is what makes the phishing list below a licence question rather than a convenience. The class has two limbs: **a shipped weight must be MIT** (or dual-licensed with MIT taken), and **its training data must be D0 or D1**. The second limb is the hard one — no inspected third-party model card documents an MIT-cleared or commissioned corpus ([DL 2.1–2.21][DL], [MA 6.1, 6.19][MA]) — and it is why Izcal's policy is to train and ship its own three Stage 1 models.*

**Training-data classes (ML-D3).** Every data item gets its class before it can reach the trainer; per-item records carry `item_id`, source and retrieval date, an SPDX licence expression, a hash of the stored licence text, D-class, consent and release ids, subject flags, an annotator record without raw worker identity, PII flags, retention date and review status. A build fails if a shipped model's ledger digest is missing or mismatched (ML-20).

| Class | What it is | Trains a shipped model? | Licence position | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D0** | Commissioned: Izcal's own photography, recordings and synthetic renders, or work-for-hire with signed releases | Yes | Izcal owns it or holds a recorded licence | **Allowed** (ML-D3) |
| **D1** | MIT, or dual-licensed with MIT as one choice | Yes | MIT option taken | **Allowed** |
| **D2** | CC0 1.0 or public domain | Only under Q-ML-9 | Imposes no conditions but is not MIT | **Conditional: Q-ML-9 open (recommendation yes, as a named exception recorded in the ledger)**; admits Common Voice, Project Gutenberg and US federal documents |
| **D3** | CC BY 4.0 | Evaluation only unless Q-ML-10 says otherwise | Attribution, not MIT; annotating an image does not license the image | **Conditional: Q-ML-10 open (recommendation no)**; class 3 while evaluation-only |
| **D4** | Research, non-commercial or no licence | No: evaluation only | Allowed by OD-L2 for tests, never for training a shipped model | **Class 3** (test-only). COCO, Visual Genome, Flickr30k, Open Images, LAION and the other image sets at image level; IAM; SROIE, DocVQA, the veraPDF corpus; Common Crawl and Wikipedia for training; YouTube and podcast audio |

**What the bundle carries.** The compiled bundle contains the URL expressions and their provenance only — not the source's text, database or code. Every bundle carries a **NOTICE / LICENSE-DATA file** and matching header metadata naming each source, its licence and the attribution text, because the bundle is public and may be redistributed; the in-app source page is additional, not a substitute. If the audit rejects a term, that source is dropped and the gap published; if it rejects every feed term, PH-D2 (c)'s crawl-only floor applies. The audit's clearance is a condition precedent, not a legal conclusion: no target assumes a source it has not cleared, and no target assumes PH-D14 (b).

- **Q-L1. - **Q-L2. rustls's own dependencies (2.2).** **Decided: OD-L3, option (b)** (own TLS stack, not the recommendation). Option (b): OS trust stores (NW-D5 (b)). **Recommendation: (a)**, because it is the only route that both meets the rule and keeps one verifier on every OS, but staff it before NW-M2 and seed it from CA certificates and audit reports Izcal reviews itself. ## Findings while applying the decisions (19 October 2026)

- **RustCrypto's elliptic-curve and RSA crates are not MIT-only.** Each crate was resolved alone with `cargo tree` (cargo 1.99.1, crates.io index of 19 October 2026). With no `subtle` in the graph: `sha2` 0.11, `sha1` 0.10, `hmac` 0.13, `hkdf` 0.13, `aes-gcm` 0.11, `chacha20poly1305` 0.11, `blake2` 0.11, `ml-kem` 0.3.3, `ed25519` 3 (types only). With `subtle` (BSD-3-Clause): `p256` and `p384` 0.14 (through `elliptic-curve` 0.14, `crypto-bigint` 0.7, `ff` and `group` 0.14, `sec1` 0.8, `primefield`) and `rsa` 0.9.10 (stable); `rsa` 0.10 is a release candidate and was not assessed. The older 0.10-line crates (`digest` 0.10, `aes-gcm` 0.10, `hmac` 0.12) also link `subtle`, so the pins must stay on the current lines. Consequence, under OD-L3's "anything else non-MIT": Izcal also writes P-256, P-384 and RSA verification, on `fiat-crypto` field arithmetic (networking NW-D23). `cmov` and `ctutils` (RustCrypto utilities) are Apache-2.0 OR MIT.
- **The dalek crates** (`curve25519-dalek` 5.0.0, `x25519-dalek` 3.0.0, `ed25519-dalek` 3.0.0) are BSD-3-Clause, as 2.1 said; `ed25519` 3.0.0 (signature types) is Apache-2.0 OR MIT.
- **Arti** is MIT OR Apache-2.0, so licence does not decide NW-D6 (c); the spec's other reasons do.
- **Unicode-3.0 in build-time crates.** `unicode-ident` ((MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND Unicode-3.0) appears under proc-macro crates; `tools/deny.toml` allows `Unicode-3.0` (OD-L1).
- **The batch-4 rows are provenance rules and conditions, not clearances (21 October 2026).** Phishunt is the only phishing feed cleared to ship today; CERT Polska and URLAbuse wait on counsel's MIT-redistribution finding, and Phishing.Database waits on freshness. In class 6, no third-party weight set is cleared: the Izcal-trained weights are the shipping plan, and Q-ML-9, Q-ML-10 and Q-ML-11 remain open, so D2, D3 and the third-party-MIT fallback are conditional. Neither addition creates a class 2 item, so the class 2 totals above are unchanged.
