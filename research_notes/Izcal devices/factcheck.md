- **I-2 quotation.** "A compromised renderer must not be able to open files, sockets, devices, PCIe config space or GPU MMIO. Everything goes through IPC to a broker." Exact at `HGBA 2 browser architecture spec.md` line 157. The MD-D10 video-only exception is the only recorded exception and is correctly stated.

## What this means for draft 0.2

The 18 Wrong findings split into three groups. **Amendment destinations** (F3–F7, F10, F12–F16) are the largest group: the content of the amendments is mostly right, but several rows point at sections, targets or IDs that do not hold the claimed material, so applying them mechanically would edit the wrong part of the corpus or collide with an existing target. **Numbers and statuses** (F1, F2, F8, F20, F21, F24) would misstate a footprint decision, a staffing base, an OS API's platform, a venue count, a team label and a revision number. **Sourcing** (F9, F11, F17–F19, F22, F23) would leave an unsourced surface claim, a wrong quotation, an unchecked date and two conflicting incident counts.

The highest-risk single item is **F1**: the footprint spec records the Windows ~74 MiB† ceiling as an owner decision, and the devices spec calls it "not accepted". A reader of 6.16 would think the device work reopens an accepted budget; it does not. The second is **F2**: 607–799† looks like a full-strength total, but its 584–768 base is not in C-6 and C-6 is a Stage 1 statement. The third is **F5**: "new SH-17" would overwrite an existing replay target. The fourth is **F8**: the OS-interface table calls an Apple framework "Android only". The fifth is **F6/F7**: two amendment rows land on an accessibility target and a cost target respectively.

## Ten findings that matter most

1. **F1** — Windows ~74 MiB† is accepted (A-SH-39), not "not accepted"; correct 6.16 and 9.5.5.
2. **F2** — C-6's 584–768† base is unsupported and C-6 is Stage 1; 607–799† is not a full-strength total.
3. **F3** — Five Stage 1 amendment locations are wrong (process map, crates/layers, milestones, totals, track index).
4. **F4** — Shell 6.5/6.6 do not own the prompt queue or ledger fields; both are in 6.4.
5. **F5** — "New SH-17" collides with the existing "Recordings replay the UI" target; use SH-35.
6. **F6** — DT-8 is accessibility, not recording start; the recording-start decision is DT-D2.
7. **F7** — DET-2 is the substrate-cost target, not the recorded-input rule; DET-1 is.
8. **F8** — Core NFC is iOS/Mac Catalyst, not Android; the row contradicts its own "Apple frameworks" label.
9. **F11/F13/F14/F15/F16** — The 3.4, 6.3, 6.15, 5.2, 5.3 and 3.5 citations point at the wrong note rows; the claims are mostly right, the evidence is not.
10. **F19** — BlueBorne and BrakTooth counts differ between the history and security notes; the spec repeats one without flagging the conflict.

## Limits and note-quality findings

- **The notes themselves need three corrections**, which draft 0.2 should not copy: `history_postmortems.md` 3.9 says WebHID is "eight years after proposal" (the spec's "six years" is arithmetically correct: September 2018 to September 2024); `gaps_opportunities.md` 5.4 says "shell 6.5/6.6 record prompts" (the prompt ledger is shell 6.4); and `history_postmortems.md` 9.6 / 9.3 conflict with `security_permissions.md` 2.5 / 2.2 on BrakTooth and BlueBorne (F19).
- **No live URL was fetched.** The 404 and status assertions are as of the notes' 21 October 2026 access; if draft 0.2 wants them as present-tense facts, it should date them.
- **The specification changed during the pass.** The pinned snapshot is the one checked. If a later revision has already fixed one of these rows, record it as "already fixed" rather than re-fixing it; the section-level evidence above still identifies the defect.
- **No third-party source was read.** The check is internal to the corpus, as M-1 part 3 and the brief require. Where a note grades a claim Medium or Low, that grade is carried into this report rather than upgraded.
