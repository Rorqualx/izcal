## Open questions

- **Forced synchronous layout prevalence** on real pages (9.5): needs an Izcal corpus measurement (count flushes forced by script per page load).
- **INP phase split** in the field (4.8): the only large figure found is a vendor page whose method is unclear; needs a LoAF-based measurement on Izcal's corpus or a published CrUX breakdown.
- **Use-counter semantics** (8.7, 8.8): the meaning of each Chrome counter is inferred from its name; whether "DocumentAll" counts every access, including feature detection, and what "AnyMutationEventListenerAdded" counts after the Chrome 127 removal, needs black-box tests.
- **WPT comparability** (8.5, 8.6): an aligned run (one WPT SHA for all five engines) and per-test rather than per-subtest rates are needed before quoting rankings.
- **Ladybird and Servo self-reports**: their newsletters' WPT figures were not read; wpt.fyi data was used instead.
- **Custom element and listener tooling** (5.4, 5.11): absence was inferred from documentation; a hands-on check of current Chrome, Firefox and Safari DevTools is needed.
- **State of HTML 2025 publication date** and survey totals: only per-question counts and percentages were read.
- **Agent benchmarks** (7.7): WebArena figures are from 2023–24; current success rates of 2026 agents were not measured here.
