## Open questions

1. **Dedicated worker creation cost** (ms to first message, MB resident) in Chrome, Firefox and Safari on a fixed device: no published number found. Needs a black-box benchmark (e.g. `new Worker` + echo, 100 runs, `performance.now()` and process RSS).
2. **Web service worker idle timeouts** per engine (Chrome's 30 s / 5 min are documented only for extensions; Firefox and Safari not found in prose). Black-box: register a SW, go idle, poll with a `fetch` to detect a cold start.
3. **Cost of a cross-process iframe today**: Reis et al. measured Chrome 69 in 2018. Needs a fresh black-box (memory per OOPIF, `postMessage` round trip same-process vs cross-process) on current Chrome and Firefox.
4. **WebKit site isolation status**: no published WebKit statement found this session (row 1.18 is Low). Black-box: count WebContent processes for a page with cross-site iframes in Safari 27.
5. **Fission memory overhead and process counts in the field**: Mozilla's posts give none. Needs a published Mozilla figure or a black-box measurement.
6. **Origin-keyed by default in the spec**: Chrome treats `?0` as an opt-out and origin-keys by default (2.6); the WHATWG text fetched today does not. Whether a WHATWG change is pending, and what Firefox and Safari plan, needs checking in the WHATWG HTML issue tracker.
7. **Son and Shmatikov 2013 counts** (receivers found, receivers with bad or missing origin checks): paper not read; needs the full PDF.
8. **localStorage cross-process latency**: synchronous `getItem` on a store held in another process; no published number. Black-box: time first and steady-state `getItem` in each engine.
9. **BroadcastChannel and SharedWorker partitioning in Safari**: not confirmed this session.
10. **bfcache hit rates per engine**: Chrome publishes blockers (3.2) but not an overall hit rate on the pages read; Firefox and Safari publish neither.
11. **Origin-Agent-Cluster Chrome version**: web.dev says Chrome 88, BCD says Chrome 90 (row 2.4). Black-box or release-note check.
12. **Memory Saver discard delay and Android discard policy**: not published; only measurable black-box.
