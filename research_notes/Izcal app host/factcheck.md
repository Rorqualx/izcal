## Summary

**Counts by verdict.** 65 rows: **Confirmed 43**, **Corrected 17**, **Overstated 5**, **Unsupported 0**, **Unverifiable 0**. **Must-fix: 12** (F1, F2, F29, F32, F33, F36, F38, F39, F42, F43, F44, F60); the other 5 Corrected rows are Recommended (F10, F24, F27, F34, F58).

**The five most consequential findings.**

1. **F29 — the supply-chain statistic is two different studies fused into one.** [DU 4.6] is Sonatype's 1,233,000+ cumulative malicious packages; [DU 4.5] is DIMVA 2020's 174 packages with an **average** 209 days to public detection. The spec calls it a **median** and attaches it to the 1.2M corpus. This is the evidence base for the directory's name rules and for "names are an attack surface"; both corrections are exact and should be applied in §3.4 and §6.7.
2. **F42 — WARG is described as signature-less, but WARG signed its package-log entries with publisher keys.** [SP 5.4, 5.7] say so directly, and the spec itself says the opposite at §6.12. The false clause weakens the "no standard signature format" argument by making the historical prior art look weaker than it was. wkg/OCI are the digest-only systems.
3. **F32/F33 — the simulation speedups are misreported.** "VOPR-1000" is 1,000 CPU cores, not 1,000×; the measured acceleration is ~700×. FoundationDB is ~10:1, not inside the 700–1,000× range. TigerBeetle's VOPR and FoundationDB are process-level simulators; only Antithesis is whole-VM with a simulated CPU. The determinism/recording section's comparison ladder needs the replacement sentences, not a number tweak.
4. **F38/F39 — two security lessons are attached to the wrong bug.** CVE-2026-47218 is runwasi/containerd-shim-wasmtime's AOT-cache trust-boundary flaw, not "Wasmtime's"; CVE-2026-27572 is a `wasi:http` header-field panic whose advisory lesson is "panicking in a WASI implementation is a DoS vector", not a hostile-cache attack. These support the no-precompiled-code rule and the host-call fuzzing rule, so the rationale should be corrected even though the design decisions survive.
5. **F2 — the Phase 4 gate requires two native implementations, not "a second".** [SP 6.1] and the BA article both say two or more Web VMs. The correction does not remove Izcal's engine from the argument, but the sentence as written understates the gate by one implementation and slightly inflates the significance of a single prototype.

Two further mechanical corrections matter out of proportion to their size: **F36** ("0.3.2 is three weeks old" — it is 8 days) and **F1** ("AH-1 to AH-14" — the targets run to AH-16). The three citation misattributions (F43, F44, F60) are mechanical but are exactly the kind of error a later reader would use to check the security argument, so they are Must-fix.
