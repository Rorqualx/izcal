# Owner decision record: C-6R, the two-ledger staffing statement

Decided 21 October 2026. The owner, on the research in `research_notes/Agentic engineering economics/` (four evidence strands plus the synthesis), adopted the two-ledger staffing statement proposed there.

## The decision

- **Ledger A** — the corpus's people and engineer-year figures (ledger C-6, Stage 1 8.3) — remains the **published, human-derived planning and audit basis**. Nothing in it changes.
- **Ledger B** — the agentic envelope of the synthesis (per-activity-class compression: implementation 1.2–2.0x; verification, measurement, craft, legal and design 1.0x or worse; compressed effort about 1,700–3,400 engineer-years†; 3.1–6.1M agent-hours; 15–31T processed tokens; $6M–$350M at October 2026 prices; Stage 1 exit floor about 40–60 months set by the measurement calendar and decision cadence) — is adopted as an **internal planning artifact** (Q-AE-1: internal). It is not the published statement.
- **Reversal trigger.** At the first real milestones per activity class (FP-M0, GR-M0, JS-M1, the first phase-8 cycle on agentic output), actual agent-hours, tokens, rework rate and wall-clock are recorded per class. If two consecutive components measure outside Ledger B's envelope on the same side (faster or slower), the class ranges are restated from the measurements, not the literature, and this record is reopened.
