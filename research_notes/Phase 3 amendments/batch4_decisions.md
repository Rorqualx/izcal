# Phase 3, batch 4: record of the owner's decisions of 21 October 2026

Written 21 October 2026. Most of the work lands on branch `spec/phase2c`; the security-scope amendments land on `spec/security-scope` and the coding-standard amendments on `spec/cs-amendments`, each in its own worktree and published when that branch merges. This file records what the owner decided on 21 October 2026 and where each decision is applied. It is a decision record, not a research note: it cites the documents it changes, and every change it names carries its own dated revision line in the document it lands in.

## 1. Every batch-4 recommendation is accepted

The owner accepted, in full and as written, every recommendation in the decision sections of the three batch-4 specifications:

- **`reports/Izcal ML service.md` §9.4, ML-D1 to ML-D12** (spec now draft 0.3). Notable consequences: **ML-D1 closes ledger G-15** — the spec owns the ML service, `hg-ml*`, the Stage 1 models and the two grants' semantics, while HGBA-2 4.5 keeps only its device-access rule and gains a replacement anchor. **ML-D2** fixes the Stage 1 model set at image descriptions (ML-IMG), OCR (ML-OCR) and captions (ML-ASR), sized at ≤ 200 MB†, ≤ 80 MB† per script and ≤ 150 MB†; HGBA-2 4.5's translate, summarise and prompt APIs are Stage 2 under the AI grant, WebNN stays behind a flag and is not shipped. **ML-D3** sets the licence regime: Izcal trains and ships its own three models under MIT and a D0–D5 data ledger, non-MIT weights never ship, and a third-party MIT weight needs an explicit owner provenance exception (Q-ML-11); it is conditional on Q-ML-12's funded corpus plan at ML-M0 and on the 25,000-image pilot. **ML-D4** keeps the two gates independent — `accessibility.model` authorises S_A only, `ai.enabled` authorises Stage 2 and agent tasks only, enabling downloads at that moment and disabling deletes, policy may force off and lock but never force on. **ML-D5** is one shared, sandboxed, network-less service with no page-facing inference path, no cross-site cache, one job at a time, a 30 s† idle exit, and `hg-ml-main` built always with generative tasks behind feature `ai`; it is conditional on HGBA-2's fourth I-1 exception, which the accepted decision grants. **ML-D6** sets canonical and fast numeric modes with integer-first kernels. **ML-D7** makes `.hgmodel` declarative, validated and threshold-signed, verified fail-closed before the loader sees it. **ML-D8** records outputs as values, never records audio or images, and mandates `numeric_mode`. **ML-D9** is CPU-first, with NPU paths only later through an OS ML runtime and only on a measured 1.5× win. **ML-D10** puts zero user-downloadable weights in the installer and sets the footprint and energy baselines. **ML-D11** is the `ml` IIP domain with hashes only. **ML-D12** requires model cards, the data ledger, the accessibility-quality rubric, the leakage check, an independent audit and the EU AI Act marking analysis.
- **`reports/Izcal shell.md` §9.4, SH-D1 to SH-D30** (spec now draft 0.3). Notable consequences: **SH-D1 closes ledger G-21** — the shell is a first-class track at 168–243 engineer-months† (6–8 steady-state, peak 10–12) plus 1.5–2.5 design/research FTE, replacing Stage 1 8.3's 1–2 row. **SH-D2** puts prompts in separate, broker-owned OS windows drawn by a dedicated prompt renderer, never an in-window trusted region; **SH-D3** makes the Windows UI host always on for content windows, TSF and the AT adapters while the broker keeps every prompt window and consent decision. **SH-D4** is one jitless shell renderer per profile; **SH-D5** forbids a UI framework; **SH-D6** seals the build image; **SH-D7** makes every capability a typed endpoint. **SH-D8 closes Q-SG-6** — history and bookmarks are one storage-service store with two catalogues, schema owned by the shell. **SH-D10** makes UI recording an explicit, navigation-scoped mode that extends DT-D2's recorded unit, with a separate content-free diagnostic ring. **SH-D11** is the local prompt ledger, 90 days†, user-clearable, never uploaded. **SH-D12** accepts a prompt only from broker-hit-tested OS input, visible and unoccluded for ≥ 500 ms†, with no synthesized input in any class. **SH-D13** moves the clipboard Paste confirmation to the prompt renderer. **SH-D16** keeps AI off with zero shell surfaces. **SH-D17** makes notifications Stage 2 and has Stage 1 deny honestly. **SH-D22** is the native five-action recovery window. **SH-D24** is WCAG 2.2 AA via WCAG2ICT, UAAG 2.0 and the ARIA APG with an AT lab pass each release. **SH-D26** makes containers the identity model, four named defaults, read-only in Stage 1. **SH-D27** keeps one tab strip in Stage 1. **SH-D28** keeps Ring A extension surfaces Stage 2 and shell-owned. **SH-D29** makes the shell network-free with opt-in telemetry and per-crash opt-out reports. **SH-D30** makes the shell inspectable through `shell.*` IIP domains. Crate rules G200–G209 are registered and enforced.
- **`reports/Izcal phishing protection.md` §9.3, PH-D1 to PH-D15** (spec now draft 0.3), with **PH-D14 resolved as its recommendation (a)**. Notable consequences: **PH-D1 closes ledger G-22 and answers Q-CB-5** — reputation is local, not a cloud lookup, and not a seventh content-blocking list; **PH-D2** ships only MIT-compatible list data plus Izcal's own observations, with non-MIT feeds test-only or exception-only. **PH-D3** is URL-level matching with host-suffix and URL-prefix expressions, URL feeds never widened to a host, and an allowlist with a build gate. **PH-D4** makes the verdict a pure function of `(url, bundle_hash, surface)`, pinned per navigation. **PH-D5** enumerates the surfaces; **PH-D6** makes the warning a full-page prompt-renderer surface in GR-D28's OS layer, with the URL and evidence shown, per navigation and no permanent bypass. **PH-D7** checks the final URL and every redirect hop for downloads, with malware blocking and phishing warning. **PH-D8** refuses agent navigations, fetches and downloads with a typed error, with no warning and no override. **PH-D9** applies the content-blocking list operation's governance with a named, published source-level exception: feed entries are auto-accepted under source vetting and a 2.5%† sampling audit, while two-person review is kept for allowlist, human, exception and top-10k entries. **PH-D10** sends no hit, warning or verdict telemetry. **PH-D11** records every check and replays it exactly. **PH-D12** is a 15-minute† manifest poll with version-addressed diffs, a daily† full sync and a seed bundle. **PH-D13** leaves command-text warnings to ED-D11 and keeps URL checks here. **PH-D15** is one local check record behind every surface, 24 h† bounded and never uploaded. Crate rules G190–G199 are registered.

Each spec's decision section carries the stamp "**Owner decision (21 October 2026): every recommendation below is accepted.**", in the form `reports/Izcal JavaScript engine.md` §9.4 uses, and each has a dated revision line (0.2 → 0.3, a notable revision under `CONTRIBUTING.md`).

## 2. PH-D14 is resolved as recommendation (a)

The owner's resolution is recorded explicitly in the phishing spec's §9.3, the licence audit and the ledger, not inferred from the blanket acceptance:

- **The MIT-only rule applies to code and data.** Phishing list data ships only if it is MIT, CC0/public domain, or under a permissive term counsel confirms permits MIT redistribution, with a NOTICE.
- **Phishunt (CC0) ships now.**
- **CERT Polska and URLAbuse ship only if the licence audit confirms** their terms permit MIT redistribution with a NOTICE; the audit and its NOTICE decide the shipped source set.
- **PhishTank, URLhaus and the Tier 2 commercial feed are test-only** unless the owner later grants a named exception (b), recorded against HGBA-2 2.3.2 and the brief. No target assumes (b).
- **The crawl-only floor (c) remains designed** and is not the shipping plan; it is the fallback if the audit rejects the remaining terms.
- **No existing row authorises shipping the feeds**: OD-L2 is test-only, OD-L6 is standards data, and OD-L5/LA 2.5 builds Izcal's own lists. The licence audit carries the feed table as provenance rules and conditions, not clearances.
