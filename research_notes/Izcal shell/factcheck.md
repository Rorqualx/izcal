# Izcal shell — phase 8 fact-check

**Method.** I read the phase-7/8 method in the first-principles-spec skill, the binding brief, all 942 lines of `reports/Izcal shell.md`, and all six research notes in this folder. I checked each load-bearing date, version, percentage, count and quoted phrase against the note and, where it was reachable, against the linked primary source; I recomputed the staffing and FP-1 arithmetic; scanned the ID ranges (SH-1..SH-34, SH-D1..SH-D30, SH-M0..SH-M6, Q-SH-1..Q-SH-12, G200..G209, T1..T13); resolved every relative link after decoding `%20`; scanned for email addresses, money figures and third-party source links; and checked every licence claim in section 7 against the notes, the M-1 table and, for two crates, crates.io.

I fetched Bilogrevic et al. (USENIX 2019), von Zezschwitz et al. (padlock), WebAIM Million 2026, Pew (2019), Apple Support 105100 and the crates.io pages for `insta` and `intl_pluralrules`. Four primaries were blocked or unusable and are marked **Unverifiable**, not confirmed: the Mozilla 2019 notification post (HTTP 403), the Mozilla settings post (HTTP 403), StatCounter (connection refused) and Chromium issue 40196304 (sign-in wall). I did not read or cite any third-party source code, headers, commits, diffs or code reviews. The notes' `chromium.googlesource.com` and `firefox-source-docs.mozilla.org` links are prose documentation; one commit-pinned documentation URL is flagged below. I did not edit the spec or any existing file.

Verdicts: **Confirmed** (matches note and, where fetched, primary); **Corrected** (the substance stands but a number, pointer or label needs changing); **Unverifiable** (primary blocked, or only a single secondary source); **Contradiction** (two parts of the spec, or the spec and a source, cannot both be true); **Internal error** (arithmetic, ID, cross-reference or internal-consistency fault). Severity: **Critical** (blocks the binding rule or the design), **Major** (changes a target, decision or headline claim), **Minor** (wording, label, pointer or completeness).
