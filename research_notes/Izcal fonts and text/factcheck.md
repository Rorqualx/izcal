# Izcal fonts and text: fact-check (phase 8)

Checked 19 October 2026 against `reports/Izcal fonts and text.md` (draft 0.1) and its seven research notes, by a checker who wrote neither. Primary sources were re-read directly. These include the Project Zero 0-day sheet (exported as xlsx and parsed), four P0 root-cause analyses, the Chrome, Microsoft Learn, W3C, Unicode and Web Almanac pages, Adobe TN #5177, the Unicode 18.0.0 test files (downloaded and counted), the wpt.fyi run summaries (recomputed), GitHub repository metadata and commit messages, and the Izcal specs on this branch plus `spec/security-scope` and `spec/coding-standard` (read with `git show`). No web search was used. No personal data was sent in any request.

Verdicts: **Correct**; **Partly** (the substance holds, but a number, scope, citation or wording is off); **Wrong**; **Unverifiable** (the source could not be read, and no other source was found). **Must-fix (FC-M#)** marks a wrong or misleading fact that a decision (TX-D1 to TX-D18) depends on. **Recommended (FC-R#)** marks everything else that needs a change.

**Counts.** 100 claims checked: 73 correct, 22 partly correct, 3 wrong, 2 unverifiable. 3 must-fix, 24 recommended.
**Links.** All 14 relative links resolve (11 in the spec, 3 in the notes, including the note link to `../Izcal resource footprint/compact_engine_data.md`). Every reference-style label (`[HP]` … `[DT]`) is defined. 267 distinct external URLs (37 in the spec, the rest in the notes) were requested with `curl -L`. 259 returned HTTP 200, 33 of them only on a slower, sequential retry (W3C and lists.w3.org rate-limit parallel requests). 7 are behind bot walls or intermittent: flutter.googlesource.com (403), forums.macrumors.com (403), petri.com (403), eweek.com (403), iso.org/standard/87621 (403), src.opensuse.org (403), and crrev.com/536535 (200 once, then 503). These were not counted as dead. **1 is dead:** `https://blues.cs.berkeley.edu/?p=152` (Fifield and Egelman) timed out on three tries. Its paper is at `https://www.bamsoftware.com/papers/fontfp.pdf` (HTTP 200, read). The content of 63 sources was read to check the claims below.
