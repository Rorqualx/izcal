# Izcal resource footprint: fact-check (phase 8)

Checked 19 October 2026 by an independent fact-checker who did not write the spec. Target: `reports/Izcal resource footprint.md` draft 0.1 (file as of 01:33). Method: every external link was requested over HTTP; every number, OS-API behaviour, date and "abandoned because" claim that a decision FP-D1…FP-D12 or a target FP-1…FP-33 depends on was checked against the primary page text (downloaded and searched, not snippets). Claims not on the decision path were sampled. Nothing in the spec or any other file was edited.

Verdicts: **Correct** = the source says it; **Partly** = substantially right but a qualifier, scope, number or citation is wrong or missing; **Wrong** = the source says something materially different; **Unverifiable** = no primary text reachable this session (stated why).
