**Implications:** EDPB 2/2023 para. 33 puts D-S4's crash uploader, the update check and the telemetry client inside ePrivacy Art. 5(3). Opt-in telemetry is fine. Opt-out crash reports need either the "strictly necessary" exemption, which no guideline settles, or real consent, which a pre-selected "send" is not (*Planet49*). The low-cost fix that keeps D-S4's sample: keep the per-crash prompt but make sending an affirmative click with nothing pre-selected, or obtain legal advice that the exemption applies, before the first EU binary. The update service should carry no per-install ID (TUF needs none), and server logs should drop IPs quickly.

## Open questions for legal advice

1. **AB 1043 and browsers.** Is a desktop browser downloaded from Izcal's own site an "application" "that can access a covered application store or download an application" (§ 1798.500(c))? If so, from 1 January 2027, must Izcal request the signal at each launch, or once at install?
2. **AB 1856.** Was it signed by 30 September 2026, and does its open-licence exemption ("an operating system or application under license terms" permitting copy, redistribution and modification) cover an MIT-licensed browser whose official binaries carry a trademark?
3. **What the signal obliges.** Once a signal is "actual knowledge" (§ 1798.501(b)(2)), does it make COPPA apply to crash reports or telemetry from an under-13 user, and is switching both off for under-18 brackets sufficient?
4. **Texas and Utah for Stage 2.** What must Izcal, as a Play developer, do under Utah SB 142 and Texas SB 2420 (consent checks, notice of "significant changes")? The Texas appeal outcome matters.
5. **ePrivacy and D-S4.** Does the Art. 5(3) "strictly necessary ... service explicitly requested" exemption cover (a) update checks, (b) crash-report upload? If not, is a per-crash prompt with nothing pre-selected valid consent, given *Planet49*?
6. **Legal basis for crash data.** Is Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interest available for minidumps that may hold URLs and page strings, and does that processing require a DPIA under Art. 35?
7. **Representative and DPO.** Where will Izcal be established? If outside the EU and UK, are Art. 27 (EU and UK) representatives required for update, crash and sync services, and is a DPO required under Art. 37?
8. **Retention.** Is SEC-35's 30-day minidump retention† defensible, given no DPA has published a period?
9. **Transfers.** If ingestion or sync runs on US hosting, is the DPF enough while C-703/25 P is pending, or should Izcal host EU data in the EU?
10. **CCPA.** Is Izcal (or its operating entity) a "business" under Civil Code § 1798.140, given its legal form and that it neither sells nor shares data?
11. **Export.** Confirm that Izcal's ML-KEM hybrid and other algorithms are not "non-standard cryptography", and whether OFAC rules limit free download in sanctioned countries.
12. **Terms.** Does an MIT-licensed browser need terms of use at all for the binary, or only for services; and what warranty and liability text is needed in EU consumer law beyond MIT's disclaimer?
13. **UK Children's Code.** Does the ICO Age Appropriate Design Code reach a browser as an "information society service likely to be accessed by children" through its update or crash services? Not researched.
