# Izcal WebCrypto: phase 8 design critique

Independent design critic, 20 October 2026. Reviewed against: HGBA-2 2.3.3 (M-1 2.3.2/2.3.3, P1–P6, I-1), Stage 1 design (2, 3.1, 3.3, 3.4, 8; G1–G9), networking spec 0.3.3 (6.5, 6.16, 6.21, NW-49..53, NW-D23, Q-NW-14, 9.3/9.5), JavaScript engine spec 0.4.1 (6.3, 6.9, 6.12, 6.14, G6), DOM spec 0.2.1 (6.8, 6.10, 6.11, 6.15, 6.17, 6.18, DM-D6/8/12/18, G95, 9.5), resource footprint spec 0.4 (8.2, FP-1/2/32/34), DevTools spec 0.3.1 (DT-D1/D2/D5, G10/G11, 9.5), Phase 3 ledger (G-18, G-19, A-NW-5, A-CON-1), and the six research notes.

**28 findings: 6 High / 13 Medium / 9 Low.** No M-1 or licence violation found (section at the end). The spec's ownership decision (WC-D1), its amendments discipline, its † marking, and its flagged-claims section are in good shape; the problems concentrate in the recording/replay story (WC-D8/WC-8), the storage path (WC-D6), one false isolation claim in the placement argument, and the schedule.

## Engine-source and licence items

**None found.** Specifically checked and clean:

- **No engine or library source as evidence.** Every historical and implementation claim traces to prose classes (W3C/NIST/RFC texts, papers, vendor documentation, maintainer posts, issue-tracker discussion); the Fiat-Crypto honesty citation is its README prose (M-1 part 3's prose-when-no-code-file-opened rule); other engines appear only as black-box differential signals and published measurements (P6-compatible, sources linked).
- **No non-MIT dependency proposed.** The four new pins (`ctr`, `cbc`, `aes-kw`, `pbkdf2`) are RustCrypto MIT OR Apache-2.0; the no-`subtle` tree check is prescribed per NW's process rather than claimed as done, which is the correct form. `fiat-crypto`'s tri-licence with an MIT choice matches NW's accepted pin. The differential oracles (Wycheproof, ACVP, black-box peers) are the test-only class OD-L2 allows.
- **No measured-value claim without a source or †.** The five flagged claims in 11.2 are honest; spot-checked numbers (Web Almanac percentages, npm downloads, Firefox verified-crypto speedups, PuTTY/Wycheproof figures) match the research notes and their grades. One carry-over nit: the "twelve operations" of 4.2 corrects the research note's "six" typo — the spec is right and the note should be fixed by its owner.
- **No template-lean.** Lessons are drawn as ideas; Chrome's WebAuthn panel, rr and the verification stack are cited as evidence and precedent, not shapes to reproduce.
