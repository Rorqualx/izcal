# Izcal feature inventory audit: fact-check

Compiled 24 October 2026. Confidence: **High** = primary source read and the claim checked in its text; **Medium** = primary snippet or vendor claim without data; **Low** = secondary source only.

M-1 note: no third-party source code was opened. No code files, commits, diffs, code reviews or code-repository data files were read. That includes the Public Suffix List data file and the ETSI EN 304 617 repository that a search returned. Only regulation text, vendor and licensing-body prose pages, and news coverage were read. The EUR-Lex text of the CRA came back without its annexes, so Annex III was read from a reproduction of the Official Journal text (springlex.eu).

Claims checked come from `os_integration_operations.md` (PO-2.9, PO-2.10, PO-4.7, PO-4.15, PO-15.5, PO-15.9, PO-15.10, PO-16.1, PO-16.5), `web_platform_apis.md` (section 14 implications), `css_graphics_media.md` (CG-12.6 to 12.8, CG-13.6 to 13.15) and `browser_product_features.md` (BF-2.4), plus the matching `map_*.md` rows.

**Implications for Izcal:** Before any EU release, Izcal needs: a 24 h / 72 h / 14-day reporting runbook to the CSIRT coordinator and ENISA; a support period of at least five years; and a Class I conformity plan. The reporting duty also covers any build already on the EU market. Not checked: whether Izcal counts as a "manufacturer" (commercial activity) or as an open-source steward under Art. 24.

## 5. European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882)

| # | Claim (PO-15.10) | Verdict | What the source says | Source | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5a | Applies from 28 June 2025; covers hardware, OSes and services; a browser is only indirectly in scope | **Confirmed** | Art. 2(1) products placed on the market after 28 June 2025: consumer general-purpose computer hardware **and operating systems**, self-service terminals, consumer terminal equipment for electronic communications and audiovisual media, e-readers. ## Summary of corrections to apply

- **BF-2.4, PO-15.5 and map group G:** an EU search-engine choice screen is **not** a legal duty for Izcal. It binds only gatekeeper browsers (Chrome, Safari). Reframe these as product choice and as eligibility for the Apple and Android browser choice screens.
- **CG-13.10 (VP9) and CG-13.2:** Google's grant covers only Google's implementations, so an independent VP9 decoder is not covered. Sisvel runs a VP9 pool. VP9 needs legal review.
- **CG-12.7:** Chrome 155 is already stable (about 6 October 2026), on a two-week cadence. JPEG XL is on in Chrome.
- **Privacy Sandbox "Chrome 144–153":** unverified. Use milestones from Chrome Platform Status, or drop them.
- **PO-15.9:** add Class I item 2, the Art. 32(2) conformity route, the 24 h / 72 h / 14-day reporting chain via ENISA's single reporting platform, Art. 69(3) coverage of products already on the market, and the five-year minimum support period.
- **PO-15.10:** raise to High. "Browser" does not appear in the EAA.
- **PO-4.15:** name the licence as Unicode License v3. **PO-4.7:** the MPL-2.0 attribution is secondary only.
